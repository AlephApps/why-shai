Harden vs SGA FTA comparison

90 seasons of 30+ PPG. Where do SGA's free throws rank?

Of the 90 times in NBA history a player averaged 30+ ppg, 52 of those seasons had 10+ FTA per game. Shai's 8.8 FTA places him 66th out of 90. He is literally below average for the 30+ PPG club.
